Known Faults — Tesla Model 3
Build Quality and Bodywork
MONITOR · 2019 on · Get a quote
What Car? says the core electric drivetrain remains exceptionally reliable, but the surrounding build quality frequently draws criticism. Owners identified a recurring trend of bodywork faults, specifically uneven panel gaps and thin paint that chips easily. In the What Car? Reliability Survey, owners also report interior rattles. Carbuyer says Tesla has been known for quality issues around fit and finish, although there are promising signs of improvement. Auto Express says owners love almost everything about their Model 3s, especially now quality has improved.
Infotainment Software Glitches
MONITOR · 2019 on · Get a quote
What Car? says software glitches in the central infotainment screen, which it calls the car’s primary control hub, can lead to frozen displays and faulty warning lights.
Steering Wheel Without Stalks
CHECK · Some cars · Get a quote
What Car? says the facelifted Model 3 has a new steering wheel without stalks, but the indicator did return in 2025 after customer feedback. It adds that even adjusting the door mirrors requires you to delve into the touchscreen.
Owner Survey Results
MONITOR · Some cars · Get a quote
What Car? says the Model 3 offers a mixed experience for reliability, according to real-world owner feedback in its annual Reliability Survey. Carbuyer reports that a shockingly high 37.9% of Model 3 owners experienced a problem with their car in the first year of ownership, according to its Driver Power customer satisfaction survey. Driving Electric says the Model 3 is one of the safest EVs you can buy, but Tesla’s reliability record leaves a lot to be desired. Auto Express says the Model 3 has been voted the best car to own in the 2026 Driver Power customer satisfaction survey.
DVSA Safety Recalls
DVSA’s recall data lists several Model 3 recalls, including these.
On certain vehicles that are running firmware release 2022.8 and that experience a loss of low voltage system support the audio subsystem may become unavailable including the use of eCall functionality.
The rear-view camera harness may fail causing no image in the centre display.
The curtain side airbags may not deploy correctly.
The front suspension lateral link may be insufficiently torqued.
Front seat belt top loop may not be secured correctly.
The brake calliper bolts may not be secured to the correct specification.

Known Faults — Tesla Model Y
Build Quality, Paint and Boot Seals
MONITOR · 2022 on · Get a quote
What Car? says many owners praise the Model Y for its mechanical dependability, reporting very few motor or battery faults, but the car is frequently let down by inconsistent build quality. It lists misaligned panel gaps, poor paint quality and faulty rubber seals that can lead to water ingress in the boot. Autocar, covering the 2021-2024 car, also names poor-quality rubber seals and panel gaps as a cause of water in the boot.
Software Glitches and Phantom Braking
MONITOR · 2022 on · Ask a Tesla service centre
What Car? says the tech-heavy Model Y can suffer from software glitches, including “phantom braking” and unresponsive touchscreens. If a car you are looking at has been doing either, ask the seller what Tesla has said about it.
Infotainment and Touchscreen
MINOR · 2021-2024 · Ask a Tesla service centre
Autocar says a laggy infotainment system, Bluetooth audio connection glitches and a yellow band showing across the touchscreen are often caused by software issues. Try the screen and the Bluetooth connection on your test drive.
Charging Port
MONITOR · 2021-2024 · Get a quote
Autocar says slow or intermittent charging and failure to charge are common problems typically caused by issues with the charging port. Plug the car in before you buy and make sure it starts charging and keeps going.
Battery and Drive Unit
CHECK · Before you buy · Check the warranty
What Car? says owners report very few motor or battery faults. Autocar reports that Tesla says on average its packs retain 85% capacity after 200,000 miles, which is Tesla’s claim rather than a finding of Autocar’s. Auto Express says the battery and drive unit warranty is eight years or 120,000 miles, so work out how much is left on the car you are viewing.
DVSA Safety Recalls
DVSA’s recall data lists several Model Y recalls, including these.
On affected vehicles one or both reverse lamps may fail to switch on when the driver selects reverse gear due to a loss of continuity in the rear lamp circuit.
On affected vehicles a firmware feature that allowed for high-speed sampling of the right front window motor encoder was not enabled bringing vehicles out of compliance with the relevant regulations for Auto-reversing Requirements.
On a small percentage of affected vehicles upon vehicle power up the car computer board may experience a short resulting in the loss of rear view camera functionality.
On affected vehicles operating with a software release prior to 2023.38.4 an overvoltage breakdown in the steering rack may overstress motor drive components on the printed circuit board.
On affected vehicles upon detection of a malfunction with the tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS) the TPMS tell tale does not persist between drive cycles where the vehicle is off or asleep between the drive cycles.

Known Faults — Tesla Model S
This page covers the Model S. What Car? writes about the 2014-2021 hatchback, Auto Express about the car from 2014 on, and Autocar’s nearly-new buying guide picks out early cars (pre-2016) for battery checks.
Infotainment Screen
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says issues with the infotainment system were common on the Model S (2014-2021), with frozen screens and slow responsiveness frequently cited.
Electric Motors and Drive Unit
HIGH · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says other problems have involved faulty electric motors that have needed to be replaced. Autocar’s guide says some early cars suffered poor bearing lubrication, so you should listen for unusual noises. It also says to accelerate hard, feeling for vibration suggesting the front driveshafts have failed.
Battery
HIGH · Any miles · Get a quote
Autocar says to give the battery a health check at a Tesla workshop, because early models (pre-2016), in particular, can suffer degradation issues or failure. Auto Express, reviewing the car from 2014 on, says that so far the battery packs of even the oldest examples are refusing to degrade significantly. Autocar also says to inspect the battery carrier for signs of impact damage with the car on a ramp.
Brake Discs
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? lists warped brake discs among the problems on the Model S (2014-2021). Auto Express says the brake discs can suffer from corrosion or deposits that lead to juddering. Autocar’s advice is to be sure there is no judder through the pedal when braking.
Door Handles and Bodywork
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? lists door handles that fail to pop out as a driver approaches. Its owners have also told it about broken exterior door handles and other bodywork problems, as well as some faults with exterior lights. Autocar says to check you are happy with the sometimes iffy panel fit and alignment, and says any damage is likely to be expensive and, because of the relative lack of industry expertise, troublesome to repair.
Climate Control, Steering and Suspension
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says problems have been reported that relate to the climate control, steering and suspension systems. Autocar says to scrutinise the suspension for damage and failure.
Cooling Warning Light
HIGH · Any miles · Get a quote
Autocar says a cooling light could mean low coolant or a failed pump, both serious.
Damp Patches in the Boot
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Autocar’s buying guide says to feel for damp patches in the boot.
Reliability Record and Owner View
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says the Model S has one of the highest fault rates in the last survey it appeared in, with 44% of the cars it was told about going wrong. Autocar says there are signs of gradual improvement, but overall Tesla has a dreadful record for reliability. One Carbuyer owner says material quality is mediocre, that the car is shockingly badly put together and that it is not reliable.
Warranty
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Autocar says every Model S comes with an eight-year, unlimited-mileage warranty covering its battery pack, while the car itself gets a four-year/50,000-mile warranty.
