Known Faults — BYD Atto 3
Range Estimate Near Empty
MONITOR · Test cars · Get a quote
What Car?’s long-term test car began slowing down on its test track with an indicated 20 miles of range remaining, even with the driver’s foot to the floor, and got home on 4% charge. Parkers’ testers ignored the 0% battery charge on their long-term test car and pushed on until the range suddenly showed zero and the motor stopped. Parkers says inaccurate range estimation led to an emergency three-pin charge.
Key Fob Connection
MONITOR · Test car · Get a quote
What Car? says its long-term test car sometimes loses communication with its key fob. It says this means the driver can’t unlock the car remotely, and there is no way to lock it.
Tailgate and Infotainment
MONITOR · Test car · Get a quote
Autocar’s 2024 long-term test car listed an electric tailgate malfunction and an infotainment crash among its faults. Autocar says the car informed its driver that evening that it was having a software update, which cured the fault in the process.
Collision Warning Alerts
MINOR · Test car · Get a quote
Autocar says its long-term test car’s collision warning sensor appears to think the car is going to crash into every parked or oncoming car, and therefore chimes incessantly.
Assisted-Driving Systems
MONITOR · Euro NCAP test · Get a quote
Carwow reports that BYD’s assisted-driving system was the only one given a ‘Not Recommended’ rating in Euro NCAP’s test. Carwow says Euro NCAP found several problems with the Atto 3’s Intelligent Adaptive Cruise Control, and that the speed assistance system failed to interpret road signs correctly.
DVSA Safety Recalls
Any DVSA safety recall on an individual Atto 3 shows up when you enter its registration in our

.
Reliability Surveys
What Car? says the Atto 3 is too new to have featured in its Reliability Survey, and BYD as a brand didn’t rank in the brand section, having only arrived in the UK in 2023. Auto Express says that in the most recent 2026 Driver Power customer satisfaction survey, BYD, as a brand, has risen from the doldrums and featured mid-table in the best manufacturer rankings. It ranked 16th out of 30 brands.
Warranty
Motoring Research reports that owners of BYD cars will get an extended eight-year EV battery warranty, now valid for 155,000 miles. Auto Express says BYD includes a six-year manufacturer warranty for the Atto 3 Evo, while the battery pack is covered by an eight-year/155,000-mile warranty.
Buying a Used BYD Atto 3
What Car? says the car has regular over-the-air software updates, so make sure these have been carried out. Auto Express says dealer coverage is building, but it is not yet at mainstream rival levels.

Frequently asked questions
Is the BYD Atto 3 reliable?
The Atto 3 is too new for What Car?'s Reliability Survey, and BYD as a brand didn't rank in its brand section. Auto Express says BYD, as a brand, ranked 16th out of 30 brands in the most recent 2026 Driver Power customer satisfaction survey.
What faults have BYD Atto 3 long-term test cars had?
Parkers' long-term test car pushed on until the range showed zero and the motor stopped. The electric tailgate malfunction and infotainment crash were on Autocar's 2024 long-term test car. What Car?'s long-term test car sometimes loses communication with its key fob.
What did Euro NCAP say about the Atto 3's assisted-driving system?
Carwow reports that BYD's system was the only one given a 'Not Recommended' rating. It says Euro NCAP found several problems with the Atto 3's Intelligent Adaptive Cruise Control, and that the speed assistance system failed to interpret road signs correctly.
What is the BYD Atto 3 battery warranty?
Motoring Research reports that owners of BYD cars get an extended eight-year EV battery warranty, now valid for 155,000 miles. Auto Express says the Atto 3 Evo's battery pack is covered by an eight-year/155,000-mile warranty, with a six-year manufacturer warranty.
