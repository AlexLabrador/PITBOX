Known Faults — Porsche 911
The faults below are grouped by generation: the 996 (Parkers and Autocar), the 997 (What Car?, Carbuyer and Auto Express), the 991 (What Car? and Auto Express) and DVSA’s recall data. Each section names its source.
996 Engine and Intermediate Shaft Bearing
HIGH · Any miles · Get a quote
Parkers, reviewing the 1997-2004 coupe, calls this the famous 996 issue: failure of the intermediate shaft bearing, which can take the engine with it. It says the problem only affects Carrera and Carrera 4 models, because the Turbos and GT cars use different engine designs, and that no-one really has any idea how many cars have suffered a catastrophic incident. Parkers adds that it can hit a 996 of any age, though the earlier dual-row bearings are thought to be stronger.
Autocar’s used buyer’s guide for the 996 says the early 3.4-litre cars are more frail than the post-2001 3.6s. It says they develop cracked cylinder heads, dropped cylinder liners and cracked intermediate driveshafts, all completely unpredictably. Autocar’s guide also says it has heard of scrapped engines at as little as 15,000 miles.
996 Oil Leaks, Suspension and Rust
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Parkers says oil leaks can be tricky to trace on the 996 because there is a lot of under-body cladding, but weepy engine seals are common, particularly the rear main seal, known as the RMS. It also tells buyers to look at the coolant. Its overall verdict is that there are well known engine issues, that the suspension components wear out rather quickly and that the cars are starting to rust now, too. Parkers warns that repairing a rusty 911 is unlikely to be cheap, as there will almost certainly be more corrosion to find once you begin.
997 Engine Failure on Early Cars
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car?, covering the 2005-2012 coupe, says engine failure is not unheard of with Gen 1 997s, although equally it is not as common as made out on some internet forums. Its warning signs are a blackened exhaust tip on the left exhausts only and a ticking sound from the engine at idle. It says Gen 2 cars didn’t suffer from the same problems and are proving to be very robust if correctly maintained.
Carbuyer’s 2004-2012 buying guide says that in a few cases, pre-2009 911s have been found to suffer engine failure. It gives the potential signs as a ticking noise when the engine is idling and/or blackened tailpipes, and it says later-built 911s aren’t affected.
997 Brakes, Coil Packs and Rust
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express says the steel braking system’s discs on the 997 are known to corrode, especially on rarely used cars, and that this is not an issue with the optional ceramic brakes. What Car? says a misfiring engine may simply be the result of faulty coil packs, a relatively cheap fix, or something more serious. It also says rust isn’t an issue on 997s, so if you see any it is most likely a sign of a poor accident repair.
991 Reliability
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? readers said the 2012-2019 911 has proven to be largely reliable, but it is not without some issues. A few owners reported problems with the car’s electronics, including faulty sensors and occasional warning lights appearing without clear causes. There were also mentions of minor oil leaks which, while not widespread, were costly to fix.
Auto Express says that although very costly problems can crop up, they’re unusual. It says a small number of owners have reported issues with the 991’s electrics, engine, gearbox, brakes and suspension, but generally the consensus is that this is a reliable car. The dual-clutch PDK is more popular, it says, but it tends to be less reliable. It adds that air-con drain pipes can come adrift on pre-facelift cars, leading to a coolant leak in the footwell. For the 2015-2018 car, Auto Express’s review says the 911 mixes solid build quality with robust mechanicals, so it should prove reliable.
Safety Recalls
CHECK · Any miles · Ask a Porsche dealer
DVSA’s recall data lists many 911 recalls, including these.
The wheel mounting with central screw connection may not meet the required specifications which can lead to damage and breakage of the central lock bolt. DVSA marks this recall as a stop-drive recall and says not to use the vehicle until further notice.
On affected vehicles the wheel attachments using the central lock may not meet the required specifications.
The screw connection of the pressure input rod of the brake booster may not have been tightened according to regulations.
Threaded connections of the upper control arms at the rear axle may not be tightened with the specified tightening torque.
The screws retaining the fuel collection pipe(s) may shear under stress.
One or more engine con rod bolts may fail and lead to the crankcase being damaged and in the worst case fire.
A fuel pipe quick connector located under the vehicle could be contacting a heating pipe.
A bearing flange on the parking brake lever is likely to break.

Frequently asked questions
Is the Porsche 911 reliable?
It depends on the generation. What Car? readers said the 2012-2019 car (991) has proven largely reliable but is not without some issues, and Auto Express says costly problems are unusual on it. What Car? says Gen 2 997 cars didn't suffer from the engine problems of the Gen 1 cars, while Parkers calls the intermediate shaft bearing the famous 996 issue.
What are the common problems with the Porsche 911?
On the 996 (1997-2004), Parkers names intermediate shaft bearing failure, weepy engine seals such as the rear main seal, suspension wear and rust. Carbuyer says pre-2009 997s have in a few cases suffered engine failure. For the 991, What Car? readers mention electronics and minor oil leaks.
Has the Porsche 911 been recalled?
Yes. DVSA's recall data lists many 911 recalls, covering things such as wheel attachments with a central lock, the brake booster, rear upper control arms, con rod bolts and fuel pipes. Enter the registration in our [recall check](/car-recall-check) to see whether a particular car has work outstanding.
What should I check on a used Porsche 911?
What Car? lists a blackened exhaust tip on the left exhausts only and a ticking sound from the engine at idle as warning signs on Gen 1 997s. Carbuyer strongly advises an inspection by an acknowledged 911 expert before any money changes hands on a privately sold 997. Auto Express says the steel discs on a 997 are known to corrode, especially on rarely used cars.

Known Faults — Porsche Cayenne
These faults come from What Car?, Autocar and Parkers.
Air Suspension
HIGH · Some cars · Get a quote
Parkers’ buying article says the air suspension is prone to leaks and eventual failure, and that the telltale sign is a sagging car and a lumpen ride. It says to expect big bills for the suspension airbags or compressor. For the Mk1 (2003-2009), Parkers’ review says suspension airbags can be expensive, particularly the rear ones. In What Car?’s owner reports for the 2010-2017 car, a handful of owners mentioned costly repairs to air suspension components. Autocar’s buying guide says to check that air-suspended cars sit level.
Air Suspension Compressor
MONITOR · From around 70k miles · Get a quote
Autocar’s buying guide says the compressor can fail from around 70k miles.
Front Lower Wishbone Arms
CHECK · Some cars · Get a quote
Autocar’s buying guide says front lower wishbone arms can be problematic.
Plastic Coolant Pipes
CHECK · 2003-2009 · Get a quote
Autocar’s buying guide says plastic coolant pipes on 2003-2006 V8s can crack and weep. Parkers’ review of the Mk1 (2003-2009) says plastic cooling pipes can fail, and that this applies to the Turbo models as well.
Centre Prop Bearing
MONITOR · 2003-2009 · Get a quote
Parkers’ review of the Mk1 (2003-2009) says a common failure is the centre prop bearing, which will cause vibration and rumbling when it begins to fail.
Electrical and Engine Management Faults
MONITOR · 2010-2017 · Get a quote
What Car?’s owner reports for the 2010-2017 car say several owners praised the robust build quality and reported few serious faults, even on higher-mileage petrol and diesel models. A handful also mentioned costly repairs to air suspension components and engine management systems, and some readers highlighted persistent issues with electrical systems, including faulty warning lights and intermittent sensor failures.
DVSA Safety Recalls
DVSA’s recall data lists several Cayenne recalls, including these.
On affected vehicles the screw connection of the high-pressure pump was not implemented in accordance with the technical specifications.
On affected vehicles there’s a possibility the upper trailing arms on the nearside front axle do not meet required specifications.
There is a possibility that a crack formation could occur on the engine cylinder bores.
A possibility that lock nuts were installed on the rear axle trailing arms that were not manufactured according to the required parameter field.
Before you buy, put the registration into our 
recall check

Frequently asked questions
Is the Porsche Cayenne reliable?
In What Car?'s Reliability Survey the 2018-present Cayenne finished 9th out of 11 cars in the luxury car class, with an overall score of 81.7%. What Car? says most Cayenne owners reported satisfaction with their vehicles, experiencing few significant issues.
What goes wrong with Porsche Cayenne air suspension?
Parkers says the system is prone to leaks and eventual failure, and that the telltale sign is a sagging car and a lumpen ride. It says to expect big bills for the suspension airbags or compressor. Autocar says the compressor can fail from around 70k miles.
Do Porsche Cayenne coolant pipes fail?
Autocar says plastic coolant pipes on 2003-2006 V8s can crack and weep. Parkers says plastic cooling pipes can fail on the Mk1 (2003-2009), and that this applies to the Turbo models as well.
What did owners report on the 2010-2017 Cayenne?
What Car? says several owners praised the robust build quality and reported few serious faults, even on higher-mileage petrol and diesel models. A handful mentioned costly repairs to air suspension components and engine management systems.
Has the Porsche Cayenne been recalled?
Yes. Among several Cayenne recalls in DVSA's data is one where there is a possibility that a crack formation could occur on the engine cylinder bores. Enter the registration in our [recall check](/car-recall-check) to see whether a car has an open DVSA safety recall.

Known Faults — Porsche Macan
The faults below come from reviews of the petrol and diesel Macan and from a few notes on the Macan Electric. Autocar’s review covers 2014-2018, What Car?’s covers 2014-present and Auto Express’s guide covers 2014-2025.
Suspension Springs
HIGH · 2014-2018 review · Get a quote
Autocar says the front and rear springs can corrode prematurely and snap. What Car? reports that owners noted premature corrosion of suspension springs, and lists it among the mechanical issues that are rarer but can be costly. Look at the springs when you inspect a car, and get a quote for any work.
Transfer Box
HIGH · Petrol and diesel Macan · Often handled under warranty
Autocar says skipping gears or a juddering or vibration from the gearbox could mean the transfer box is on its way out. What Car? says a more serious “judder” may signal a failing transfer box, a known fault often handled under warranty. One owner of a 2015 car told Carbuyer the transfer box fails regularly around 30K miles and the car feels as if driving over a rumble strip in low gears. That is one owner’s experience, not a survey result.
V6 Petrol Oil Leaks
MONITOR · V6 petrol models · Get a quote
What Car? says owners noted potential oil leaks on V6 petrol models. It puts oil leaks in the group of mechanical issues that are rarer but can be costly. Look under the car and around the engine of any V6 petrol, and ask the seller about leaks.
Ignition Coil
MINOR · 2014-2018 review · Get a quote
Autocar says a rough idle or a misfire is often caused by a faulty ignition coil. If a car you are viewing idles roughly or misfires, ask for the coil to be checked before you buy.
Diesel Particulate Filter
CHECK · Low-mileage diesels · Get a quote
Auto Express says low-mileage diesel models can suffer faults with their particulate filters. Ask a diesel seller what use the car has had and whether any warning lights have come on.
Electrics and Infotainment
MONITOR · Any miles · Sometimes a dealer software update
What Car? says the most frequent complaints involve non-engine electrics and infotainment glitches, which sometimes require dealer software updates to fix. It adds that some owners have reported issues with non-engine electrics, so be sure to check these. Auto Express says the navigation system in pre-facelift models doesn’t cope all that well with postcodes, but the later set-up is much better.
Macan Electric Reliability
MINOR · Macan Electric · Warranty cover
Auto Express says that with the Macan Electric being a recent arrival, it’s difficult to accurately assess any potential reliability woes. Carbuyer says Porsche performs exceptionally in terms of owner satisfaction, although reliability could be better. Driving Electric says Porsche has a solid reputation for reliability, and the Macan should follow suit. Carwow gives the industry standard warranty on the battery, motor and electrical components, and Auto Express says the battery is guaranteed to maintain above 70 per cent capacity for that period.
DVSA Safety Recalls
DVSA’s recall data lists several Porsche Macan recalls, including these.
On the affected vehicles it is possible that during the production process the airbags in the front seats were not fastened to the seat backrest in accordance with specifications
During regular conformity of production tests by the supplier a deviation at a component of the passenger’s airbag unit has been identified (listed under Macan Electric)
The software version of the engine control unit does not correspond to the software version that should be on the vehicle
On the affected vehicles it is possible that the screw connection of the seat belt mount to the rear left and/or right seat was not carried out in accordance with the specifications
On affected vehicles certain software parameters used in Type Approval were not used in production

Buying a Used Porsche Macan
Carbuyer says customer service is generally very good, although servicing costs are high and repair costs may be a concern once the standard warranty has expired. Autocar says to make sure the PDK auto gearbox has been serviced regularly.
On a test drive, try every electrical function and the infotainment screen, and feel for judder or skipping in low gears. Check which version of the Macan the advert describes, as the petrol and diesel cars and the Macan Electric have different notes above. Insurance costs depend on the car and the driver, so get quotes by registration.

Known Faults — Porsche Taycan
This page covers the Taycan. What Car? writes about the saloon (2020-present), Parkers and Auto Express about the car from 2019 onwards, and one Auto Express long-term test is of a Taycan 4S Cross Turismo.
Air-Con and Infotainment
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car?, in its reliability page for the Taycan saloon (2020-present), says a hefty 46% of the Taycans in its survey went wrong, with owners citing the air-con and infotainment system as the main trouble spots. What Car? notes that these, rather than anything more serious, were the main trouble spots.
Software Glitches
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says owners reported issues with the car’s software, including system crashes and glitches in the infotainment and navigation systems. Auto Express, in its Mk1 (2019-date) buyer’s guide, says the software needs to be up-to-date, to maximise range and minimise running problems with the infotainment, as well as the car’s electrical systems, any of which can play up.
Waits for Repairs and Service Costs
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car? says all remedial work in its survey was done for free, but two-thirds of the stricken cars were out of action for more than a week. It lists long wait times for software updates and repairs, as well as high service costs, among the common complaints. Parkers, on the Taycan from 2019 onwards, says everything from the servicing to the consumable components (largely tyres, but also eventually brakes) will be on the pricey side.
Reliability Ranking
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
In the latest What Car? Reliability Survey, the Taycan placed fourteenth out of seventeen models in the electric car class. Parkers says the Taycan has suffered reliability issues. Electrifying says there have been some reliability issues.
Optional Onboard Charger Fault
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express ran a Taycan 4S Cross Turismo as a long-term test car. On that car, an electrical glitch after an overnight thunderstorm upset the onboard charger, an optional three-phase unit. After some investigation, the charger turned out to have developed a fault, and it had to be replaced.
Options to Check When Buying
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express says the options list was extensive, so you should pin down what infotainment, trim, brakes, wheels, paint and seats are fitted. It also says to check what driver-assistance systems the car has, because some were extra-cost options.
Warranty and Battery Cover
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Parkers says the Taycan has a three-year, unlimited mileage warranty, and that the battery is covered for eight years but only 100,000 miles. Auto Express, writing about the Mk1 (2019-date), says all Taycans have eight-year battery cover, guaranteeing 70 per cent of its original capacity.

Known Faults — Porsche Boxster
The faults below are grouped by generation. Each section names its source and the years that source gives.
986 Intermediate Shaft Bearing
HIGH · Post-2000 cars · Get a quote
Autocar’s used buying guide for the 986 (1996-2004) says that cars made after 2000 with a single-row bearing are more prone to failure than earlier cars with a dual-row. It says bearing noises at the rear of the engine on a post-2000 car could indicate failure of the intermediate shaft bearing (IMS). Autocar adds that relatively few cars suffered failure of the IMS, but if it does let go, the engine will be destroyed. The 2013 specialist advice in evo calls bearing-type noises from the rear of the engine of more concern.
986 Bore Liners and VarioCam Chain Guides
HIGH · Pre-2002 cars · Get a quote
Autocar says that on a pre-2001 986, emulsified oil could indicate cracked bore liners, and it says to have a specialist do a bore scope check. It also says pre-2002 cars especially can suffer worn chain guides on the VarioCam timing chain tensioner.
986 RMS Trouble and Roof Drains
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Autocar’s 986 guide says RMS trouble can affect all 986-series Boxsters. It says the part is cheap but it is the labour that costs, since the gearbox must be removed. It also says to look for water behind the seats caused by blocked roof drains.
987 Engine
MONITOR · Early cars · Get a quote
Parkers, writing about the Roadster of 2004-2011, says this generation of Boxster suffers with many of the same issues other Porsches of this era have to contend with. It lists bore scoring, IMS bearing failure and worn suspension as things to consider carefully before making a purchase.
Autocar’s guide to the 987 (2005-2012) says cylinder scoring is rare but worth checking for on pre-DFI 3.4 S cars, and that the signs include an oily tailpipe and high oil consumption. On early 987s it says to check whether the IMS bearing has been uprated, and on early cars to check for an oil leak from the rear main oil seal.
The 987 buying guide in evo (2005-2012) says one of the key areas to check on any Boxster is around the bell housing. It says that if the bearing fails then generally the engine will need rebuilding. If the gearbox is out for a clutch change, evo says the bearing can be checked and, if there is play, replaced without removing the engine.
987 Suspension and Clutch
MONITOR · Around 40,000 miles · Get a quote
Autocar’s 987 guide says lower arm bushes tend to go at around 40,000 miles. It says knocking may be due to the rose joints on the trailing arms. It also says broken suspension springs aren’t rare, so check how the car is sitting and for unusual noises over rough roads. On the clutch, it says stiff changes and a heavy pedal suggest failure is imminent.
The evo 987 guide also says to check the coolant radiators and air-conditioning condensers at the front of the car.
981 Owner Survey
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
The RAC’s review of the 981 (2012-2016) says the normally aspirated flat six engines are reliable and charismatic units which have yet to show up any significant problems. Its survey of MK3 model Boxster owners revealed a lot of very happy people but, inevitably, a few issues. One owner reported vibrations at speed, traced to uneven tyre wear. Another reported hesitant starting and an electric parking brake issue.
718 Reliability
MINOR · Any miles · Ask a Porsche specialist
What Car?’s used Boxster reliability review says the Boxster is a generally reliable car, but it has its faults. It reports that 21% of 718 Cayman and Boxsters suffered a fault in its 2025 survey.
Auto Express, in its buyer’s guide to the 718 (2016-2025), says most of these cars are cherished and low-mileage, with problems more likely to be down to abuse than any manufacturing faults, recall glitches aside. It says to connect the car to an official Porsche PIWIS computer before buying, for a diagnostics check.
Safety Recalls
CHECK · Any miles · Ask a Porsche dealer
DVSA’s recall data lists several Boxster recalls, including these.
The transmission shift cable may have had the safety clip incorrectly fitted, or the cable sheath may be damaged, risking a roll away incident after the vehicle is placed in park (P) if the parking brake is not applied.
The carrier side parts installed on the rear axle during a certain period may not meet the required specifications.
Connecting rods that were not manufactured according to specifications may have been installed in the engines of the affected vehicles (listed by DVSA as 718 Boxster).
The cylinder bank 2 high-pressure fuel line might not be tight (listed by DVSA as 718 Boxster).
There is a possible fault within the air bag control unit.
The side airbag sensors may not be tightened to specification.
The upper part of the catch mechanism fitted to the front luggage compartment lid may not be secured to the correct specification.
The locking hook on the sunshield may not engage correctly into the lock catch on the windscreen frame.
A bearing flange on the parking brake lever is likely to break.

Frequently asked questions
Is the Porsche Boxster reliable?
The RAC says the 981's normally aspirated flat six engines are reliable and charismatic units which have yet to show up any significant problems, although its owner survey raised a few issues. What Car? says the Boxster is a generally reliable car, but it has its faults. Auto Express says problems on the 718 are more likely to be down to abuse than manufacturing faults, recall glitches aside.
What are the common problems with the Porsche Boxster?
Autocar's guide to the 986 (1996-2004) names failure of the intermediate shaft bearing (IMS), cracked bore liners on pre-2001 cars, worn VarioCam chain guides on pre-2002 cars and RMS trouble. For the 987, Parkers lists bore scoring, IMS bearing failure and worn suspension.
Has the Porsche Boxster been recalled?
Yes. DVSA's recall data lists several Boxster recalls, covering things such as a transmission shift cable, rear axle carrier parts, connecting rods, the air bag control unit and a bearing flange on the parking brake lever. Enter the registration in our [recall check](/car-recall-check) to see whether a particular car has work outstanding.
What should I check on a used Porsche Boxster?
For a 986, Autocar says to have a specialist do a bore scope check and to look for water behind the seats caused by blocked roof drains. On an early 987 it says to check whether the IMS bearing has been uprated and to check for an oil leak from the rear main oil seal. For a 718, Auto Express says to connect the car to an official Porsche PIWIS computer before buying, for a diagnostics check.

Known Faults — Porsche Cayman
Bore Scoring on Early 987 Engines
HIGH · Early 987 cars · Get a quote
evo, reviewing the 987 Cayman (2005-2012), says the Gen 2 version is the safest bet in terms of reliability, since those cars are less prone to the bore scoring that is notorious with some earlier engines. It says scoring can be identified by high oil consumption or one of the twin tailpipes being dirtier than the other. The scoring tends to occur on the second cylinder bank, and the poor seal between the bores and piston rings results in dark exhaust deposits on that side.
Intermediate Shaft Bearing on Pre-Facelift 987 Cars
HIGH · Pre-facelift 987 · Get a quote
evo adds that pre-facelift models can also be prone to intermediate shaft bearing failure. It says the DFI engine of the facelifted 987 eliminated the IMS altogether to solve the issue.
981 Electrics and Warning Lights
MONITOR · 981 cars · Get a quote
What Car?, reviewing the 2013-2016 Cayman, says some owners did encounter problems, particularly with electrical components. Warning lights illuminating unexpectedly was a recurring issue, with some drivers facing costly diagnostic fees before faults were identified. RAC Drive, covering the 981 from 2012 to 2016, came across cars that had noises in their instrument binnacles, heater blower motor problems, a glovebox that wouldn’t close, an alarm problem and a PSM failure warning light that kept coming on. One owner in its survey reported hesitant starting and an electric parking brake issue.
981 Gearbox and Running Gear
MONITOR · 981 cars · Get a quote
What Car? says mechanical reliability on the 2013-2016 Cayman was generally solid, though a few owners reported issues with the gearbox, requiring dealer intervention. One RAC Drive survey owner reported vibrations at speed, traced to uneven tyre wear. Autocar, writing about the 981 from 2012, says there are rare cases of PASM dampers leaking but calls it an easy fix when taken to a specialist.
Corrosion and Diagnostics on a Used 981
CHECK · 981 cars · Get a quote
Autocar says that if not monitored properly, corrosion can set in on certain parts such as air condensers, damaged radiators, exhaust fixings and brake parts. Its guide also says to get a diagnostic of the engine management system to check that the dashboard clock mileage is legitimate and to check for any fault codes or cases of over-revving.
GT4 Track Wear and Strut Towers
HIGH · 981 GT4 · Get a quote
evo, reviewing the GT4 (981, 2015-2016), says dampers, catalysts and dynamic engine mounts have been known to fail, and in rare instances strut towers have had catastrophic failures. It says this wouldn’t be easy to hide, but advises checking for below-par repair work to be on the safe side. Because the car has a manual gearbox and is a prime candidate for track use, evo says checking for over-rev reports is wise, as is looking for the usual brake, bearing and bush wear associated with hard circuit driving. It also says the GT4 is reliable if the car has been well maintained and cared for.
718 Bodywork
MINOR · 718 Cayman · Get a quote
What Car?’s reliability page for the 718 Cayman (2016-present) lists bodywork, at 6%, among the most common faults. It says to check the bodywork carefully for any signs of damage.
Reliability Surveys and Warranty
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
In What Car?’s annual Reliability Survey, the 718 Cayman placed seventh out of eight models in the coupe, convertible and sports car category and recorded a below-average score of 89.5%. Carbuyer says you won’t find the Cayman in the latest 2024 Driver Power survey, with too few owners responding for it to appear in the rankings. What Car? says the 2013-2016 Cayman has its faults but is a generally reliable car. RAC Drive says the 981’s normally aspirated flat six engines have yet to show up any significant problems, and its owner survey revealed a lot of very happy people but a few issues. Car Magazine, in its guide to the 981 from 2012, says reliability is good. Auto Express says only the earliest 718 Caymans are out of manufacturer warranty, although extended cover is available and worth investing in.

Frequently asked questions
Is the Porsche Cayman reliable?
What Car? says the 2013-2016 Cayman has its faults but is a generally reliable car. RAC Drive says the 981's normally aspirated flat six engines are reliable units which have yet to show up any significant problems, and Car Magazine says reliability is good for the 981. For the 718 Cayman, What Car? places it seventh out of eight in its category with a below-average 89.5%.
What goes wrong on a used Cayman?
evo says pre-facelift 987 models can be prone to intermediate shaft bearing failure, and that bore scoring is notorious with some earlier engines. RAC Drive found 981 cars with noises in the instrument binnacles, heater blower motor problems and a PSM failure warning light that kept coming on. What Car? says warning lights illuminating unexpectedly was a recurring issue on the 2013-2016 car.
How can I spot bore scoring on a 987 Cayman?
evo says it can be identified by high oil consumption or one of the twin tailpipes being dirtier than the other. It says the scoring tends to occur on the second cylinder bank, and that the poor seal between the bores and piston rings results in dark exhaust deposits on that side.
Is the Cayman GT4 reliable?
evo, reviewing the 981 GT4 from 2015 to 2016, says it is reliable if the car has been well maintained and cared for. It adds that dampers, catalysts and dynamic engine mounts have been known to fail, and that checking for over-rev reports is wise on a car that is a prime candidate for track use.
Is a 718 Cayman under manufacturer warranty?
Auto Express, reviewing the 718 Cayman from 2016 on, says only the earliest 718 Caymans are out of manufacturer warranty. It says extended cover is available and worth investing in.
What should I check before buying a used Cayman?
Autocar's guide to the 981 from 2012 says to get a diagnostic of the engine management system to check that the dashboard clock mileage is legitimate and to check for any fault codes or cases of over-revving. What Car? says to check the bodywork carefully for any signs of damage on the 718 Cayman.
