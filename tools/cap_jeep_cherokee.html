    Known Faults — Jeep Cherokee

    Reliability Survey Result

    MONITOR · 2014-present cars · Get a quote

    In its reliability review of the 2014-present Cherokee, What Car? says the car finished near the bottom in the large SUV class in its most recent reliability survey.

    Engines Familiar From Fiat Models

    MONITOR · 2014-present cars · Get a quote

    What Car? says the engines in the 2014-present Cherokee are familiar from other Fiat models, and that there have been many reported problems.

    Electrical Issues

    MONITOR · 2014-2020 cars · Get a quote

    RAC Drive, in its used review of the 2014-2020 Cherokee KL, says to look out for the kind of electrical issues that tend to afflict Fiat group cars, as this Cherokee’s engineering is mainly Fiat-based.

    Diesel Particulate Filters

    MONITOR · 2014-2020 diesels · Get a quote

    If you are buying one of the diesel models, What Car? says the diesel particulate filters are prone to clogging. RAC Drive makes the point for the 2014-2020 car, saying to look out for blocked diesel particulate filters on diesels that have been used too much in town.

    Off-Road Damage

    CHECK · 2014-present cars · Get a quote

    What Car? says there is a chance that your Cherokee has been used for a bit of off-road action. It tells buyers to check the front, rear and undersides of the car carefully to make sure no damage has been incurred.

    Navigation System Updates

    MINOR · 2014-2020 cars · Get a quote

    In its review of the 2014-2020 Cherokee KL, RAC Drive says owners tell it that the navigation system updates are difficult to implement.

    Mechanical Reliability

    MONITOR · 2001-2008 cars · Get a quote

    In its reliability review of the 2001-2008 Cherokee 4x4, What Car? says owners have criticised mechanical reliability, but that generally the Cherokee is a robust piece of kit.

    Suspension and Axles

    MONITOR · 2001-2008 cars · Get a quote

    For the 2001-2008 car, What Car? says the suspension and axles are the weakest area.

    Sunroof, Door Locks and Mirrors

    CHECK · 2002-2007 cars · Get a quote

    RAC Drive, reviewing the 2002-2007 Cherokee KJ, says to check the sunroof, door-locks and mirrors for trouble-free operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How did the Jeep Cherokee do in the What Car? reliability survey?

    What Car?, in its review of the 2014-present Cherokee, says the car finished near the bottom in the large SUV class in its most recent reliability survey. It adds that the engines are familiar from other Fiat models and that there have been many reported problems.

    What goes wrong on a diesel Cherokee?

    For buyers of a 2014-present diesel, What Car? says the diesel particulate filters are prone to clogging. RAC Drive, writing about the 2014-2020 car, says to look out for blocked diesel particulate filters on diesels that have been used too much in town.

    What does What Car? say about the 2001-2008 Cherokee?

    It says owners have criticised mechanical reliability, but generally the Cherokee is a robust piece of kit. It names the suspension and axles as the weakest area.

    What should I look at on a used Cherokee?

    What Car? says there is a chance a 2014-present Cherokee has been used for a bit of off-road action, so check the front, rear and undersides carefully for damage. For a 2002-2007 car, RAC Drive says to check the sunroof, door-locks and mirrors for trouble-free operation.
