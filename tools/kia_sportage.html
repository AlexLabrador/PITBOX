    Known Faults — Kia Sportage

    The faults below come from reviews of three periods: the 2010-2016 car, the 2016-2021 (Mk4) car, and the 2021-present car. We name the years each source gives.

    For the 2016-2022 car, What Car? reports that owners told it 56% of their diesel Sportages went wrong, compared with only 20% of petrol models. It adds that the diesel-engined cars finished last with a fairly poor score of just 66.2% overall. By build year, What Car? says cars made between 2016-2018 have proved reliable, those built between 2018-2022 have not. For the 2021-present car, it gives a reliability score of 95.90% and says most owners reported no major mechanical faults.

    Diesel DPF and EGR

    MONITOR · 1.7 CRDi diesel · Get a quote

    FixMyCar says the 1.7 CRDi diesel in some Sportage models can suffer more frequent DPF regeneration triggers and soot-related EGR valve carbon build-up, particularly on short urban journeys where the engine doesn’t fully warm up. What Car? notes that the diesel is fitted with a diesel particulate filter, which requires a high-speed run every now and then to allow it to self-clean. Ask a seller how a diesel has been driven, and watch for dashboard warnings on a test drive.

    Gearbox

    MONITOR · Older 6-speed manuals · Get a quote

    FixMyCar writes that owner surveys highlight transmission as one of the top-reported areas when things go wrong. It adds that some Sportage gearboxes, especially older 6-speed manuals, have been known to exhibit gear crunching or difficulty selecting certain gears in 3rd-6th. On a test drive, work through every gear, up and down.

    Engine Computer and Clutch

    CHECK · 2016-2021 · Warranty may cover it

    Carbuyer says there is evidence online that some Mk4 cars can suffer from engine computer problems or a juddering clutch, both of which will be covered by the warranty. That only helps if cover remains, so find out what is left on the car you are looking at. Pull away gently from a standstill and feel for judder through the pedal.

    Fuel Filters on Diesels

    CHECK · 2010-2016 diesel · Get a quote

    For the 2010-2016 car, What Car? says there have been reports of new fuel filters needed after complete loss of power on diesels. Ask a seller of an older diesel whether this has happened and what was done about it.

    ISG Stop-Start System

    MINOR · 2010-2016 · Get a quote

    What Car? says some owners of the 2010-2016 car have reported a problem with the ISG stop-start system. Check that it works when you stop at junctions during a test drive.

    Steering Feel

    CHECK · 2016-2021 · Get a quote

    Carbuyer’s reliability page for the Mk4 says odd-feeling steering might be one area to look out for. It gives no cause, so treat it as something to notice on the road. A drive on a straight road and through a few bends will show whether anything feels strange.

    Sat-Nav and Starting Electrics

    MINOR · 2016-2022 · Get a quote

    What Car? says there have been some issues with non-engine electrics on the 2016-2022 car, mostly relating to the sat-nav system (where fitted), and the starting procedure. Try the sat-nav, and start the car from cold and from warm.

    DVSA Safety Recalls

    DVSA’s recall data lists several Sportage recalls, including these. On some cars, the vacuum pump drive gear may be damaged by a mechanical failure caused by high acceleration torque during the engine starting phase. In another recall, the mesh filter inside the tandem pump may block, causing insufficient oil supply and a risk of vacuum pump failure. On some cars, the electrical circuit within the HECU may experience a short circuit condition that results in excessive current. Another recall says that on affected vehicles there may be some contamination of the brake fluid due to topping up with an incorrect fluid. Enter the registration in our recall check to see whether a car has a recall that is still open.

    Buying a Used Sportage

    Kia UK’s terms say it covers the components of a new Kia car for unlimited mileage up to 36 months, and for 100,000 miles between 37 and 84 months, from the date of first registration. Ask the seller how much of that cover is left, and read Kia’s terms and exclusions before you rely on it.

    Choose the engine with care, given the gap between diesel and petrol in What Car?’s survey. Run the registration through our recall check before you buy. Insurance costs depend on the car and the driver, so get quotes by registration.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Kia Sportage reliable?

    It depends on the car. What Car? says cars made between 2016-2018 have proved reliable, those built between 2018-2022 have not. For the 2021-present Sportage it says the car has generally proved dependable across both petrol and hybrid versions, with a reliability score of 95.90%.

    Does the Sportage diesel have DPF problems?

    FixMyCar says the most common headaches tend to be diesel emissions issues (DPF/EGR warnings on short trips), and that the 1.7 CRDi in some Sportage models can suffer more frequent DPF regeneration triggers. What Car? says a diesel Sportage needs a high-speed run every now and then to allow the filter to self-clean.

    What does the Kia warranty cover?

    Kia UK's terms say it covers the components of a new Kia car for unlimited mileage up to 36 months, and for 100,000 miles between 37 and 84 months, from the date of first registration. Read the full terms and exclusions on Kia's website, and ask the seller what cover is left on the car you are viewing.

    Are there recalls on the Sportage?

    Yes, DVSA's recall data lists several Sportage recalls. Enter the registration in our [recall check](/car-recall-check) to see whether a car has a safety recall that is still open.

    What should I check on a used Sportage?

    On a diesel, ask about warning lights and how the car has been used. On any car, change gear through every ratio, feel the clutch and steering, check the sat-nav, and make sure the car starts cleanly and the stop-start works. Get insurance quotes by registration.
