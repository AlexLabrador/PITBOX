    Known Faults — MINI Clubman

    Cooper S Timing Chain Tensioner

    MONITOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    On the 2007-2014 Clubman (R55), What Car? says Cooper S models with the turbocharged petrol engine can make a loud rattle on start-up from cold. It puts this down to a weak timing chain tensioner that fails to maintain tension over time.

    Clutch Wear

    MONITOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    What Car? says some owners have reported that the clutch can wear out quite quickly on a 2007-2014 Clubman. It advises doing some checks before you buy.

    Stop-Start Stalling

    MONITOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    According to What Car?, the stop-start system on the 2007-2014 Clubman has been known to malfunction and stall the car at junctions. It adds that dealers often struggle to diagnose the fault.

    Early Diesel Turbo

    CHECK · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    What Car? says early diesel engines on the 2007-2014 Clubman, also used in Peugeot and Citroën cars, can destroy their turbocharger with oil starvation due to excessive carbon build-up.

    Air Conditioning

    MONITOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    What Car? says it has heard numerous reports of problems with the air conditioning system on the 2007-2014 Clubman.

    Interior Rattles

    MINOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    What Car? says the interior and dashboard of the 2007-2014 Clubman have suffered from rattles and creaks.

    Windscreen Chips and Cracks

    MINOR · 2007-2014 · Get a quote

    What Car? says the Clubman’s windscreen is very susceptible to chips and cracks because of its upright position.

    Bodywork and Paint

    MONITOR · 2015-2024 · Get a quote

    In What Car?’s 2023 Reliability Survey of the 2015-2024 Clubman (F54), bodywork issues were the main trouble spot, affecting 11% of cars. Two-thirds were fixed for free, while the rest cost £100 or less. What Car? adds that if the paint on the rear barn doors is damaged, it could lead to rust problems later on.

    Flooded Front Footwells

    CHECK · Some cars · Get a quote

    A Carbuyer owner of a 2016 Clubman Cooper wrote that the front footwells started flooding and the car spent three weeks at the MINI dealership. The owner says this was traced to a faulty air-con pipe that should drain water from the unit under the car but was draining into the car.

    Dial Fizzing and Rattling

    MINOR · 2015-2024 · Get a quote

    Autocar’s long-term Clubman Cooper D, a 2016-17 car, had a dial that kept fizzing and rattling.

    Reliability Survey Results

    What Car? says the 2015-2024 Clubman ranked third out of 29 cars in the family car class, with a rating of 97.9%. As a car brand, it says, MINI proved very reliable, finishing third out of 32 manufacturers in that survey. Few issues were reported, and those that did arise were typically minor and promptly addressed by dealerships.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the MINI Clubman reliable?

    In What Car?'s 2023 Reliability Survey the 2015-2024 Clubman (F54) ranked third out of 29 family cars, with a rating of 97.9%. MINI as a brand finished third out of 32 manufacturers in that survey.

    What goes wrong on the 2015-2024 MINI Clubman?

    What Car? says bodywork issues were the main trouble spot, affecting 11% of cars. Two-thirds were fixed for free, while the rest cost £100 or less.

    What are the common problems on the 2007-2014 MINI Clubman?

    What Car? lists a loud rattle on a cold start from a weak timing chain tensioner on the Cooper S, a clutch that can wear out quite quickly, stop-start that can stall the car at junctions, rattles and creaks inside, and air conditioning problems.

    Is the MINI Clubman prone to rust?

    What Car? advises checking the rear barn doors of the 2015-2024 Clubman, because if the paint is damaged, it could lead to rust problems later on.
