Known Faults — MG MG4
The sources below report problems on the MG4 sold from 2022.
Lane-Keep Assist
Which? testing, 2024
Which? reported that the MG4’s lane-keep assistance technology twice decided that the car had left its intended lane and pulled it onto the wrong side of the road. It also reported that the lane-assist system steered the MG4 towards an oncoming vehicle. Which? said the issues couldn’t be repeated in subsequent tests, but were significant enough for it to investigate. MG told Which? it was aware of issues with the car’s lane departure warning system and that a software update would soon be announced. MG said it had developed an improved calibration of the advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), which would be rolled out as a software update. That is what MG said, so ask an MG dealer to confirm whether a particular car has had the update.
Software Glitches and Warning Lights
Owners in What Car?’s survey
What Car? says several owners reported issues with electrical faults, such as software glitches and warning lights appearing on the dashboard. A few said these problems were intermittent, leading to frustration. In What Car?’s list of most common faults, non-motor electrics came top at 15%, with sat-nav and infotainment at 7%. On a test drive, check that every warning light goes out and that the screen and driver aids behave normally.
Charging Socket and Charge Ports
What Car? owner and Autocar
One owner told What Car? that the charging socket on their brand new MG4 broke after just 10 weeks of ownership. That is one owner’s experience. Autocar lists failing charge ports among the more serious problems on a used MG4. What Car?’s fault breakdown puts the battery and charging system at 4%. Plug the car into a charger before you buy and make sure it starts charging, keeps charging and releases the cable.
Differentials Leaking Oil
Autocar
Autocar lists differentials leaking oil among the more serious problems on a used MG4. Look under the car for signs of oil and ask what the seller knows about it.
Slow Repairs
What Car? survey data
What Car? says repairs weren’t always swift, with 68% of affected cars taking more than a week to fix. In its separate figures, 80% of the afflicted cars were fixed for free, 20% of owners faced bills of £1000 to £1500, and 70% were laid up for more than a week. If you depend on the car every day, ask what cover and help you would have if it went wrong.
Interior Trim and Bodywork
What Car?, Parkers and Autocar
What Car? lists interior trim at 9% and bodywork at 7% of the most common faults. Parkers’ long-term car arrived with over 8000 miles on the clock, and Parkers called the scratch marks in the hard plastic disappointing. Autocar describes textured plastic that’s unpleasantly hard yet can be permanently scratched with just a fingernail. Autocar adds that rattling bootlids are annoying but not major reliability issues. Look over the trim and listen for rattles on the test drive.
Bluetooth and CarPlay
One Carbuyer owner
One owner wrote in a Carbuyer review that they had Bluetooth and CarPlay reliability issues since the get go. This is one owner’s experience, so pair your phone and try CarPlay before you buy.
Real-World Range
Parkers long-term test
Parkers’ long-term car, tested from June to August 2024, showed an official range of 329 miles and a tested range of 208 miles. Parkers said its overall efficiency of 3.3 miles per kWh was a bit off the pace these days. MG’s own site claims up to 338 miles for the 77kWh battery version, so ask which battery a used car has.
Reliability Survey Results
In What Car?’s ranking the MG4 came 18th out of 20 cars in the electric car class, with a reliability score of 76.1%. Bodywork and non-motor electrical issues were the main complaints from the 37.5% of MG4 owners who said their car had gone wrong. In its 2024 survey, the MG4 performed even more poorly, with a reliability score of just 63.8%, and finished last out of 18 cars in the electric car class.
DVSA Safety Recalls
DVSA’s recall data lists this MG4 recall.
On affected vehicles there is potential for MG4 vehicles to superlock whilst still in running mode when the vehicle comes to a stop

Warranty
The MG warranty terms cover 12 months and unlimited miles, or up to 7 years and 80,000 miles, whichever occurs first. From 01/01/2025 the high voltage battery is covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes sooner. MG’s terms also refer to the battery suffering a capacity drop below 70% of the original value at delivery. Read the full terms to see how the cover applies to a used car.
Buying a Used MG4
Carwow says to check carefully that all the car’s features work as they should. Work through the screen, Bluetooth, CarPlay, driver aids and warning lights, and try both a home-type charger and a rapid charger if you can. Frequently asked questions
Is the MG4 reliable?
What Car? put the MG4 18th out of 20 cars in the electric car class, with a reliability score of 76.1%. In its 2024 survey the MG4 finished last out of 18 cars with a score of 63.8%. Bodywork and non-motor electrical issues were the main complaints from the 37.5% of owners who said their car had gone wrong.
What warranty does the MG4 have?
MG's warranty terms cover 12 months and unlimited miles, or up to 7 years and 80,000 miles, whichever occurs first. From 01/01/2025 the high voltage battery is covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes sooner. Check the date of first registration to see how much cover a used car has left.
Has the MG4 been recalled?
DVSA's recall data lists one MG4 recall, for doors that could superlock. Use our [recall check](/car-recall-check) with the registration to see whether a car is affected.
Did MG fix the lane-keep assist problem?
MG told Which? it was aware of issues with the car's lane departure warning system and that a software update would soon be announced. It said it had developed an improved calibration of the advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), to be rolled out as a software update. Ask an MG dealer whether a used car has had it.
How far does the MG4 go on a charge in real life?
Parkers' long-term car had an official range of 329 miles and a tested range of 208 miles, tested from June to August 2024. Its overall efficiency was 3.3 miles per kWh.

Known Faults — MG5 EV
This page covers the MG5 EV estate. Auto Express writes about the Mk1 from 2020 on, What Car? about the 2020-2025 car, and the RAC about the MG5 EV Long Range of 2021-2022.
Infotainment and Dashboard Displays
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express, reviewing the MG5 Mk1 (2020 on), says the most common issue that owners seem to face is with the infotainment system and dashboard displays, especially with pre-facelift cars. In its owner survey, What Car? says that when issues do arise, they often involve the infotainment system. The RAC adds that you should go thoroughly over all the powered and infotainment functions of the car you are looking at.
Electrical and Software Issues
CHECK · Any miles · Get a quote
The RAC, on the MG5 EV Long Range (2021-2022), says there are not many major issues, other than a few electrical and software issues. What Car? owners spoke of minor electrical “niggles”, such as intermittent warning sounds without clear dashboard faults.
Limp Mode and the 12-Volt Battery
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express says some owners have had problems with their cars going into limp mode. A software update usually fixes things, it says, or sometimes a replacement 12-volt battery.
Dealer Service
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express notes that the MG5 has a long warranty, but says reliability and dealer service can be patchy. It adds that if problems do arise, the quality of dealer service can be inconsistent.
Reliability Survey Result
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
In the 2025 What Car? Reliability Survey, the MG5 finished in seventh place out of twenty electric cars, with a good overall score of 94.1%. What Car? says owners described a generally robust experience, earning a respectable reliability rating despite the brand’s lower overall standing. Auto Express says there are also lots of owners who have had a trouble-free experience, and its advice before buying an MG5 is to undertake a comprehensive inspection and test drive.
Warranty on a Used MG5
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
Auto Express says all MG5s came with a seven-year warranty with an 80,000-mile cap. The RAC, on the MG5 EV Long Range (2021-2022), describes MG’s warranty as a fully transferable 7 year/80,000 mile package.

Frequently asked questions
Is the MG ZS reliable?
What Car? says the ZS EV is not a reliable car. In its latest Reliability Survey the ZS EV finished in 24th place out of 27 cars in the electric SUV category, with a score of 81.8%. FixMyCar reports that in the 2025 What Car? reliability survey the ZS came 31st out of 38 small SUVs with a score of 91.6%. MG as a brand finished in last place out of 30 manufacturers in What Car?'s survey, with a score of 88.9%.
Does the MG ZS come with a long warranty?
MG's warranty terms cover either 12 months and unlimited miles or up to 7 years and 80,000 miles, whichever occurs first. For the high-voltage battery, MG lists 96 months or 100,000 miles against capacity falling below 70% of original, for vehicles registered from 01/01/2024. Read the current terms on MG's website and check which terms applied to the car's registration date.
What do owners say about the MG ZS?
Auto Express reports that the MG ZS came last of the 50 cars in the 2026 Driver Power customer satisfaction survey, and says some owners have been left reeling by troublesome tech and one too many repairs. Carbuyer says MG has performed poorly for customer satisfaction. Parkers says MG does not have the best reliability record in recent times, although the brand reports fewer problems with the latest generation of models.
Is the MG ZS EV a good used buy?
What Car? says a significant number of owners have reported frustrating electrical faults and software glitches. If you are looking at one, try it on a charger, check the infotainment screen and warning lights on a test drive, and make sure all the charging cables are supplied, because What Car? says they can be quite expensive to replace.

Known Faults — MG HS
Infotainment and Software Crashes
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
What Car?’s reliability survey found frequent electronic glitches on the HS, particularly with the infotainment system and its frequently crashing software. The RAC also mentions a few electrical and software issues, and advises going thoroughly over all the powered and infotainment functions before you buy. Ask an MG dealer whether any software updates are outstanding.
Battery Drain, Central Locking and Key Fob Faults
MONITOR · Any miles · Get a quote
The faults What Car? owners found most concerning were battery drain and central locking failures. The RAC adds that some owners have reported the car occasionally doesn’t recognise the key fob. On a test drive, lock and unlock the car several times with the fob, and ask whether the battery has ever gone flat.
Body Control Module Faults (Some 2022 Cars)
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
According to the RAC, owners of a few 2022 models have said that in hot weather some body control modules can cause problems, with windows not going down or up and mirrors not folding. Check every window and the mirror folding on a warm day if you can.
Rear Brake Disc Wear and Noisy Clutch Release Bearing
MINOR · Mileage varies · Get a quote
What Car? owners reported premature wear on the rear brake discs, and noisy clutch release bearings. Look at the rear discs for a worn lip or heavy scoring, and listen for noise from the clutch as you press and release the pedal on a manual car.
Sunroof Leaks, Automatic Wipers and Trim Fit
MINOR · Any miles · Get a quote
The RAC has heard of the automatic wipers being inaccurate and of a few sunroofs leaking, with water dripping onto the sun visors. It also notes occasional quality issues such as plastic clips breaking and panels not lining up perfectly. These are worth fixing under warranty while it still applies.
Plug-in Hybrid (EHS) Recall
CHECK · Built July 2020 to October 2022 · Ask an MG dealer
The DVSA’s safety recall covers plug-in hybrids built from 28 July 2020 to 27 October 2022 (listed as the MG EHS). The PTC ground wire may not be anchored to the body correctly, so insulation can be trapped between the wire and the body, giving a poor connection and a risk of overheating. The remedy is to check the insulation and modify it if necessary. Enter the registration in our

Frequently asked questions
Is the MG HS reliable?
Not according to What Car?'s reliability survey: out of 76 family SUVs the plug-in hybrid finished 44th and the petrol 61st, and MG finished last of 30 manufacturers. Carbuyer reports that MG also came last of 32 manufacturers in its 2023 Driver Power customer satisfaction survey. The RAC is kinder, saying there aren't many major issues other than a few electrical and software ones.
Does the MG HS come with a long warranty?
Yes. All MGs are covered for up to seven years or 80,000 miles, and the RAC describes the warranty as fully transferable, so it passes to the next owner while it is still running. Check the terms for your car's registration date and mileage.
Has the MG HS been recalled?
The DVSA lists one safety recall, for the plug-in hybrid (which it lists as the MG EHS), launched in March 2023, for cars built from 28 July 2020 to 27 October 2022. The PTC ground wire may not be anchored to the body correctly, which can cause a poor connection and a risk of overheating; the fix is to check the insulation and modify it if necessary. Enter the registration in our [recall check](/car-recall-check) to see whether a car still needs the work.
What should I check on a used MG HS?
The RAC's advice is to go thoroughly over all the powered and infotainment functions of the car you're looking at. Check that the central locking and key fob work every time, look for water marks on the sun visors under the sunroof, and on a plug-in hybrid check that the recall above has been done.
How much does an MG HS service cost?
Costs depend on the job and the garage. Get quotes from an independent specialist for an annual service or a full major service.
